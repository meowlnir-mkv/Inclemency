# Containment Breach: Inclemency

An overhaul mod for SCP – Containment Breach.
https://www.moddb.com/mods/cb-inclemency

Containment Breach: Inclemency and SCP – Containment Breach are based on the works of the SCP Foundation community (https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/).

Inclemency runs on the BlitzPRO Legacy engine, and is based off of [the SCP-CB Community Preservation Project's v1.3.12 edition of SCP – Containment Breach](https://github.com/SCP-CB-CPP/scpcb).
Inclemency is licensed under the [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/), as it is a derivative work of SCP – Containment Breach and the SCP Foundation collaborative writing project.

See Credits.txt for credits to assets used in Inclemency and the original SCP – Containment Breach's list of contributors.

Requirements:

-	[BlitzPRO Legacy](https://github.com/Euclid-Labs-Studio/BlitzPRO/releases/tag/legacy-v2.100)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Make sure to copy all .decls files from this repo's `Userlibs` folder to your Blitz3D installation's `userlibs` folder before compiling.
