
------------------------- CONTAINMENT BREACH: INCLEMENCY -------------------------
-------------------- https://www.moddb.com/mods/cb-inclemency --------------------

An overhaul mod of SCP – Containment Breach, featuring new and expanded sound systems, custom sound effects, new music tracks, custom textures, rewritten text strings, and miscellaneous tweaks & additions.

Containment Breach: Inclemency and SCP – Containment Breach are based on the works of the SCP Foundation community (https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/).
Inclemency runs on the BlitzPRO Legacy engine and is based off of the SCP-CB Community Preservation Project's v1.3.12 edition of SCP – Containment Breach.
See Credits.txt for credits to assets used in Inclemency and the original SCP – Containment Breach's list of contributors.

To play, double-click Inclemency.exe in the game folder to run the launcher. In the launcher, choose your desired resolution and window mode, then click "LAUNCH".

For a place to report issues, provide feedback, and just talk about Inclemency, join the Discord server by clicking the "DISCORD" button in the launcher.
