	Hi! Thank you for playing Containment Breach: Inclemency. I hope you've had
	a good time with it! I know I'm having a good time making it, even if I've
	hit a few roadblocks and taken hits to my morale from time to time. Working 
	on a solo project as an amateur in everything is a very daunting task.
	Inclemency means a lot to me, I've gone through thick and thin with it and I
	hope that my ambitions and effort won't be for nothing, and that my goal of
	making the original game better will be achieved.

	Thank you to everyone who has rode along with me during development so far,
	and I hope you stick around to see where Inclemency goes in the future. If
	you've had any qualms or issues with the mod, please join the Discord and let
	me know!

	Love you! <3

	- meowlnir.mkv
